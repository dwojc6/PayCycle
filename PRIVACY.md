# PayCycle Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 31, 2026_

PayCycle is a personal finance app designed to help you view accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, and forecasts in one place. This policy explains what data PayCycle accesses and how that data is handled.

## Financial Data Access

With your permission, PayCycle may access financial account information from Apple Wallet through FinanceKit, including eligible Apple Card, Apple Cash, and Savings account details, balances, and transactions. PayCycle uses this information only to show your financial activity, calculate balances, support budgeting, and provide forecasting features inside the app.

## Other Connections

If you choose to connect Lunch Money or SimpleFIN, PayCycle may access account, balance, transaction, category, and budget data provided by those services. API tokens and access URLs are stored securely in your device keychain.

## Data Storage

PayCycle stores app data on your device so the app can display recently synced accounts, balances, transactions, and settings. Sensitive credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. PayCycle does not sell your financial data.

## Data Sharing

PayCycle does not share your FinanceKit, Apple Wallet, Lunch Money, or SimpleFIN financial data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties. PayCycle does not use financial data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## User Control

You can disconnect integrations or clear stored connection data from the app's Settings. You can also manage FinanceKit and Wallet data permissions through iOS settings where available.

## Security

PayCycle is designed to keep financial data on device where possible and uses Apple platform security features such as the Keychain for stored credentials. No method of storage or transmission is perfect, but PayCycle limits access to financial data to the features you choose to use.

## Children

PayCycle is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as PayCycle changes. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.
